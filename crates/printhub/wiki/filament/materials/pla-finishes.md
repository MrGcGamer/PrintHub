PLA made for its look. It prints much like [PLA](/wiki/filament/materials/pla); the profiles
adjust temperature, cooling and flow for each finish.

## Settings

| Profile | Nozzle | Bed | Fan | Flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elegoo PLA Matte | 220 (190–240) | 65 / 60 | 60–80 | 16 |
| Elegoo PLA Silk | 230 (190–240) | 65 / 60 | 60–80 | 16 |
| Elegoo PLA Translucent2 | 220 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 100 | 15 |
| Elegoo PLA Galaxy | 220 (190–220) | 60 / 60 | 80–100 | 18 |
| Elegoo PLA Sparkle | 220 (190–220) | 60 / 60 | 80–100 | 18 |
| Elegoo PLA Marble | 220 (190–220) | 60 / 60 | 80–100 | 18 |
| Elegoo PLA Glow | 220 (190–230) | 60 / 60 | 100 | 18 |
| Elegoo PLA Wood | 220 (200–240) | 60 / 60 | 80–100 | 10 |

Silk prints hotter than plain PLA, and matte, silk and translucent flow slower. Choose the
profile at upload: slicing does not find it from a spool whose material is `PLA`.

## Wood

Wood-filled PLA carries wood particles, which raise the risk of a clogged nozzle. Prusa
recommends a nozzle of at least 0.6 mm with 0.2 mm layers for wood fills other than its own.
Parts are weaker than plain PLA, and can be stained like wood.
