ASA does what ABS does and adds resistance to sunlight, so it is the material for parts that live
outside. It warps and smells less than ABS, withstands heat up to 93 °C, resists impact and wear,
and like ABS can be sanded, glued and smoothed with acetone.

**Use it for** outdoor parts, parts near heat, and tough technical parts.

**Avoid it for** outdoor parts that stay below 80 °C, if warping is a worry:
[PETG](/wiki/filament/materials/petg) covers those and hardly warps.

## Settings

| Profile | Nozzle | Bed | Fan | Flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elegoo ASA | 270 (240–280) | 90 / 90 | 10–80 | 12 |
| Elegoo ASA-CF | 270 (240–280) | 100 / 90 | 10–30 | 18 |
| Generic ASA-CF | 260 (240–280) | 90 / 90 | 10–60 | 12 |

ASA-CF is filled with carbon fibre; read [Fibre-filled filament](/wiki/filament/materials/fibre-filled)
first.

## Printing it

- ASA gives off styrene fumes while printing. Keep the printer closed and the room ventilated.
- It warps where the part cools unevenly; large parts need the enclosure closed.
- It takes up some moisture; see [Drying](/wiki/filament/materials/drying).
