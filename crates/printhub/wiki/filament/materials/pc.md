Polycarbonate is used for safety glasses and headlight covers because it is tough, strong and
takes heat. Printable PC is blended with additives, since pure PC warps and cracks.

**Use it for** technical parts that need heat and impact resistance together.

**Avoid it for** large parts that cover much of the bed, which warp, and for a first try at
printing engineering materials: start with [PETG](/wiki/filament/materials/petg).

## Settings

| Profile | Nozzle | Bed | Fan | Flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elegoo PC | 270 (250–270) | 100 / 110 | 10–35 | 16 |
| Elegoo PC-FR | 280 (260–290) | 110 / 110 | 10–40 | 18 |
| Generic PC | 270 (240–290) | 110 / 110 | 20–60 | 12 |
| Generic PC-CF | 260 (240–290) | 90 / 90 | 20–60 | 16 |

110 °C is the most the Centauri Carbon 2's bed reaches. PC-CF is filled with carbon fibre; read
[Fibre-filled filament](/wiki/filament/materials/fibre-filled) first.

## Printing it

- Dry it before every print; Elegoo calls drying PC mandatory. See
  [Drying](/wiki/filament/materials/drying).
- Keep the enclosure closed. Rounded corners warp less than sharp ones, and denser infill warps
  more.
- Add a brim to small parts with little contact with the bed.
- It smells moderately while printing.
