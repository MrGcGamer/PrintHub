PLA is a plant-based plastic that prints at low temperatures, barely warps and holds fine detail,
from small figures to prints that fill the bed.

**Use it for** concept models, prototypes, toys, figures and decoration.

**Avoid it for** anything outdoors, near heat, or under load. PLA softens above 60 °C, degrades in
sunlight, and breaks along its layers or shatters when hit.

## Settings

| Profile | Nozzle | Bed | Fan | Flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elegoo PLA | 210 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 50–100 | 21 |
| Elegoo PLA Basic | 220 (190–230) | 60 / 60 | 50–100 | 21 |
| Elegoo PLA PRO | 220 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 50–100 | 20 |
| Elegoo PLA+ | 220 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 50–100 | 20 |
| Elegoo Rapid PLA+ | 220 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 60–100 | 21 |
| Elegoo PLA-CF | 220 (190–240) | 65 / 60 | 60–80 | 16 |
| Generic PLA | 210 (190–240) | 60 / 60 | 50–100 | 21 |

Elegoo's PLA lines differ by a few degrees and in flow; pick the profile named on the spool.
PLA-CF is filled with carbon fibre; read [Fibre-filled filament](/wiki/filament/materials/fibre-filled)
before printing it.

## Printing it

PLA can leave fine strings between parts, more so when the filament is wet; see
[Drying](/wiki/filament/materials/drying). Failed prints are not garden compost:
PLA breaks down only in industrial composting above 80 °C.
