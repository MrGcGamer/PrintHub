ABS makes sturdy, heat-resistant technical parts at a low price, and is the plastic of LEGO
bricks. It can be glued with acetone and smoothed glossy in acetone vapour.

**Use it for** technical parts that meet heat or knocks, and parts to be acetone-smoothed.

**Avoid it for** parts in the sun: outdoors it yellows and turns brittle, and
[ASA](/wiki/filament/materials/asa) does better there.

## Settings

| Profile | Nozzle | Bed | Fan | Flow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elegoo ABS | 270 (240–280) | 90 / 90 | 10–40 | 18 |
| Generic ABS | 270 (240–280) | 90 / 90 | 10–80 | 12 |
| Generic ABS-CF | 260 (240–280) | 90 / 90 | 10–30 | 12 |

ABS-CF is filled with carbon fibre; read [Fibre-filled filament](/wiki/filament/materials/fibre-filled)
first.

## Printing it

- ABS gives off styrene fumes while printing. Keep the printer closed and the room ventilated.
- It warps where the part cools unevenly; it needs the enclosure closed and a warm chamber.
- Parts shrink by about 1–2 % as they cool. Test-fit a small piece before printing parts that
  have to fit something.
