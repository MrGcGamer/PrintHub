Everyone sees everyone's statistics. Click a name for that person's page.

## Periods

*Last 30 days* counts the 30 days up to now, in daily bars. *Last 12 months* starts on the first
of the month eleven months back, so the first bar is a whole month. *All time*, the default, is
in monthly bars.

## What counts as a print

A job that reached the printer, started, and ended: done, failed or stopped. Jobs cancelled
before they printed do not count, and neither do prints started without PrintHub. A failed job
that was retried and then finished counts once, as done; the filament its failed attempt used
still counts as wasted.

Print time runs from the start to the end of the print, including time paused.

## Filament

Filament is counted when a print ends, so a print still running is not in the figures yet.
Whose spool it came from is the spool's owner at that moment; a spool given to someone else
later does not move past filament.

*Worth* counts only spools that have a price. Each person keeps their chart colour in every
period; accounts after the eighth share one grey series, *Everyone else*.

## Materials

Filament per material name, as written on the spools. Names that differ only in upper and lower
case are counted together. See [Material names](/wiki/filament#material-names).

## Not accounted for

The sum of every [weigh-in](/wiki/filament/weighing#weigh-ins) in the period, by spool owner. A
negative figure means weigh-ins found more filament than PrintHub had counted, for example
because a stopped print's estimate was too high. None of it is part of anyone's printing or
balance.
