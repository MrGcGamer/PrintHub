## Where a debt comes from

Printing from another person's spool that has a price. The filament is worth
*grams × price ÷ grams on a full spool*, worked out when the print ends and fixed then: changing
the spool's price or owner later leaves it alone. A stopped or failed print costs the share it
used.

Printing from your own spool, a shared spool or a spool without a price creates no debt.

## Netting

Debts between two people cancel out. If Alex used 5.00 of Sam's filament and Sam used 2.00 of
Alex's, Alex owes Sam 3.00. Payments count against the debt, and paying more than owed turns it
around: the one who was paid now owes the difference.

Amounts carry no currency; they are in whatever currency the spool prices were entered in.

## Recording a payment

The amount field starts at the full debt; change it for a part payment. Amounts take a dot or a
comma, such as 12.50 or 12,50. The last 20 payments are listed below the balances. An admin can
delete a payment recorded by mistake, which restores the debt it paid.
